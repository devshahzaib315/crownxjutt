A few useful details about the way I work, the things I build and how we can get a project moving.

HAVE ANOTHER QUESTION? LET’S TALK ↗
01What kind of projects do you take on?+

I work on creative websites, interactive digital experiences, 3D/WebGL scenes, product interfaces and brand-led web experiences. The best fit is a project that values both thoughtful design and solid development.

02Can you build a website with 3D and WebGL?+

Yes. Three.js and WebGL can be used for interactive objects, immersive hero sections, shader effects and spatial storytelling. I also consider loading performance, mobile behaviour and a graceful fallback.

03Will my website work on mobile devices?+

Responsive behaviour is part of the build, not an afterthought. Layouts, navigation and interactions are adapted for smaller screens, with motion and 3D effects tuned to suit the device.

04How do we get started?+

Send a short message about your idea, goals, references and any preferred deadline. We can then discuss the scope, priorities and a sensible next step.

05Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?+

Yes. Projects can be discussed and coordinated remotely. Clear communication, shared references and agreed milestones help keep collaboration smooth across time zones.

06How much does a project cost?+

It depends on the scope, features, content and level of custom design or 3D work involved. Once I understand what you need, I can discuss an appropriate estimate before work begins.
